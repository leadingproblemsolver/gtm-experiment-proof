# Clara Interview Notes — MAXX GTM Engineer

These notes explain how I would describe the proof accurately in an interview.

## Opening

I'm early in my formal career, so I don't want to substitute titles for evidence. I built a small proof specifically for this role before applying: a real 10-account market wedge, current operators, source-backed signals, deterministic routing, qualification rules, and a kill condition.

The point is not that the targets are definitely buyers. The point is that the next experiment is explicit and measurable.

## Why MAXX?

The role is unusually aligned with how I like to work: turn growth into a system, make routing and experiments explicit, and judge work by external movement rather than activity.

## Relevant experience

I have limited conventional GTM tenure. I've built and tested technical systems around evidence collection, routing, workflow reliability, APIs, automation, and operational decision logic. I've also run real outbound experiments with named operators and tracked replies.

## What did you build?

I chose recruitment as one wedge because MAXX publicly supports prospecting, follow-up, CRM, proposals, customer support, and recruitment workflows.

I mapped ten real firms with visible workflow density and current senior operators. Internal pain stays unverified until an operator confirms it.

## Why not AI scoring?

Before enough outcome data exists, precise-looking scores are mostly decoration. Start with explicit rules, log outcomes, and only introduce learned scoring when labelled outcomes can beat the simple baseline.

## Week one

Reconstruct the current funnel before changing it: sources, enrichment, routing, sequencing, attribution, and qualified-conversation baseline. Then ship one narrow experiment with a pre-declared kill condition.

## Success

Primary metric: cost per qualified conversation.

Decompose it into:

source → contact → reply → qualified conversation → pipeline

## Biggest gap

I don't have years of production GTM-engineering tenure or a long revenue track record yet. I won't hide that.

My case is that I can reason across technical and commercial boundaries, ship quickly, measure what happens, and learn from direct market evidence.

## Why take the risk?

Judge the operating behavior, not a claim. The artifact is inspectable, falsifiable, and designed to generate real outcome data.

## Closing

Inspect the artifact, challenge the assumptions, and judge how I respond to real outcome data.
