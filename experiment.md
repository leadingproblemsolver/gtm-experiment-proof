# Bounded GTM Experiment

## Goal

Test whether owner-led / mid-market recruitment firms with visible multi-region or high-volume workflow density produce qualified conversations for MAXX.

## Why this wedge

MAXX publicly sells companions for prospecting, follow-up, proposals, CRM, customer support, recruitment, and getting paid.

Recruitment firms visibly contain repeated lead, candidate, client, handoff, and follow-up workflows. That makes them useful for a fast validation experiment.

## Hypothesis

Not:

> These firms have broken workflows.

Instead:

> Public workflow density makes these firms high-information targets for testing whether follow-up, routing, CRM state, proposal/admin work, or customer/candidate communication is consequential enough to justify a MAXX companion.

## Experiment

For each account:

1. Use one public signal.
2. Contact one current operator.
3. Ask one historical-behavior question.
4. Do not pitch until a real workflow/failure is described.
5. Route the result to:
   - VALIDATED
   - INVALIDATED
   - WRONG_OWNER
   - LOW_CONSEQUENCE
   - COMMITMENT
6. After initial disinterest, allow one factual probe.
7. If no consequential reality emerges, kill the target.

## Qualified-conversation definition

A qualified conversation requires:

- a concrete current workflow,
- a repeated friction/failure or costly manual burden,
- an identified owner,
- willingness to take a meaningful next step.

Interest, compliments, or “sounds useful” do not count.

## Primary metric

**Cost per qualified conversation**

## Secondary metrics

- contact → reply rate
- reply → problem-validation rate
- validation → next-commitment rate
- time spent per account
- kill rate

## Kill condition

If fewer than **2 of 10** accounts expose a consequential workflow after real operator conversations, retire or materially change the recruitment wedge.

## Claim ceiling

This artifact demonstrates:

- market segmentation,
- provenance-preserving research,
- deterministic routing,
- experiment design,
- explicit qualification criteria,
- explicit kill conditions.

It does **not** prove:

- these companies are buying,
- internal pain exists,
- MAXX would close them,
- CAC would fall,
- the wedge is commercially validated.

Those require live execution.
