# Field Test Offer

## Evidence-backed outbound execution sprint

Give me **one B2B offer + one ICP**.

I will return a first **5-account execution pack** built to answer a narrower question than “can AI personalize outreach?”:

> Which accounts are actually worth spending human time on, and why?

For each account, the pack includes:

- company
- named buyer/operator
- observable trigger
- evidence/source
- observed facts
- inferred pain/workflow hypothesis
- confidence
- smallest useful intervention
- personalized opener
- CTA
- state: `READY / RESEARCH / REJECT`

## Operating rule

```text
public signal
→ evidence
→ observed vs inferred
→ confidence
→ intervention
→ READY / RESEARCH / REJECT
```

Weak accounts are allowed to die. The goal is not to force every company through “personalization.”

## What this is

A bounded, manual-first GTM research and execution test.

It is useful for:

- B2B founders doing founder-led sales
- lean GTM teams
- agencies or technical service businesses
- teams with large prospect lists but weak account prioritization
- operators who want evidence behind outreach rather than generic enrichment

## What this is not

This is **not** a claim of:

- guaranteed revenue
- guaranteed meetings
- buying intent
- product-market fit
- conversion lift

Those require external outcomes.

## First test

Send:

1. your offer
2. your ICP
3. any hard exclusions

I will run the first five accounts manually.

If the five are not materially more useful than your current prospect research, stop the experiment.

If they are useful, the next step is a 20–30 account paid execution batch.

## Proof discipline

The system preserves:

```text
BUILT != TESTED != EXTERNALLY USED != PAID
PUBLIC SIGNAL != INTERNAL PAIN
PUBLIC SIGNAL != BUYING INTENT
```

The point is to earn the right to make a better commercial decision before spending more human time.
